# Monieplans product context

Monieplans helps people plan a monthly spending limit through flexible category buckets and weekly pacing. The primary scene is quick daily expense entry on a phone, then checking availability before spending. Monthly-only reserves such as Fuel stay accessible and need no weekly setup. Users explicitly move unused allocation; preserved targets make top-ups visible in reviews. Camera and upload capture always require review before a save. AI proposals are optional and never alter plans automatically.

The approved product specification lives in PRD.md. Implemented operating surfaces are Home, Plan, Activity, Review, bucket details, and Profile/settings. The deployment remains Workers plus Pages. Maintain monetary correctness, readable merchant names, keyboard access, and calm mobile composition. Production capture and AI depend on provisioned external bindings and receipt evaluation before rollout.
